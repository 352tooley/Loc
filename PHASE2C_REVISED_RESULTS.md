# SmartPack Phase 2C-Revised — Results & Final Assessment

## Executive Summary

Phase 2C-Revised explored three paths to achieve RAM reduction beyond Phase 2B:
1.  **Tensor Sensitivity (Dense Models):** Failed. Clustering of domain-specific weights is too weak (7-9% shared).
2.  **MoE Selective Loading:** Mixed/Weak. Domain expert overlap is high (~70%). Maximum theoretical reduction ~21%.
3.  **Paged Runtime (mmap):** Failed. Default mmap behavior touches most pages during loading, resulting in RSS exceeding model size.

**Recommendation: Abandon Phase 2C.** Ship Phase 2B as the final production-ready version. The gains from 2C do not justify the architectural complexity or the risk of quality degradation.

## Detailed Findings

### 1. Tensor Sensitivity (Phase 2C-A)
We performed tensor ablation on `SmolLM2-360M` and `Qwen2.5-1.5B` to identify which tensors are critical for specific domains (Code, Math, Chat, Summarization).

- **Hypothesis:** 20-40% of tensors are shared; the rest are domain-specific.
- **Actual (Qwen 1.5B):** 6.51% shared.
- **Actual (SmolLM2 360M):** 7.03% shared.
- **Observation:** Most tensors are either "mixed" (sensitive to multiple domains) or "sparse" (low sensitivity overall). True domain isolation is rare in dense models at this scale.

### 2. MoE Selective Loading (Phase 2C-C)
We analyzed expert activation patterns in `Qwen1.5-MoE-A2.7B` (60 experts, 4 active per token).

- **Hypothesis:** Experts cluster by domain, allowing us to load only a subset.
- **Actual Overlap:** ~70% of expert-layers are common to ALL domains.
- **Max Theoretical Reduction:** 20.5% (for the Chat domain).
- **Observation:** While MoE models are sparse, the sparsity is not highly correlated with broad domains like "Code" vs "Math" in this model. Most experts are generalists or specialized at a finer granularity.

### 3. Paged Runtime (Phase 2C-D)
We tested mmap-backed lazy loading using `llama-cpp-python`.

- **Hypothesis:** OS paging will only load active weights, reducing peak RSS.
- **Actual:** RSS increased by 1757MB for a 1065MB model.
- **Observation:** The overhead of KV-cache, computation buffers, and the fact that inference touches many layers results in memory usage that exceeds the file size. True "lazy loading" that keeps the model footprint small is not achieved with default GGUF runtimes.

## Phase 2B Baseline (The Winner)

Phase 2B remains the most stable and effective strategy:
- **Routing:** 100% accuracy with SmolLM2-360M coordinator.
- **Swap Latency:** 2.97s (reduced by 21ms via Preload Agent).
- **RAM Footprint:** ~2.5GB (fits 3B models comfortably on 8GB machines).
- **Quality:** 100% escalation accuracy for high-risk domains.

## Final Recommendation

**The "8GB machine running 16GB model" goal via weight clustering is not currently viable for dense models at the 1.5B-7B scale.** 

The clustering hypothesis held in semantic simulation (Phase 1) but failed in weight-level ablation (Phase 2C). The weights of a trained transformer are more interconnected than their semantic embeddings suggest.

We will **not merge Phase 2C**. We will proceed with Phase 2B as the production baseline.
